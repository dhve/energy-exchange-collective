Read the protocol and reproducibility manifest
Synthetic households. No live customer data, meter readings, money, or actual energy transfer.
Logical network. Discovery links represent allowed contact. They do not represent physical electricity paths.
Separate stages. Provisional allocation, final commitment, and meter-supported settlement have distinct meanings. This workspace explores negotiation.
