How negotiation works

Energy is one flexible pool per home within its delivery window. There are no hourly power, device or weather limits. These logical connections do not establish grid feasibility.

A stopping result means no further admissible move under the recorded strategy and bounded rules. It does not establish a global optimum or physical delivery.

METHOD & LIMITS

A transparent, bounded experiment.

The negotiation protocol enumerates an explicit set of feasible bilateral moves. Its stopping condition is reported for every run.

Read the protocol and reproducibility manifest
Synthetic households. No live customer data, meter readings, money, or actual energy transfer.
Logical network. Discovery links represent allowed contact. They do not represent physical electricity paths.
Separate stages. Provisional allocation, final commitment, and meter-supported settlement have distinct meanings. This workspace explores negotiation.
